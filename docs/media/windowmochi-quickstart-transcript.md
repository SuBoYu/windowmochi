# WindowMochi illustrated quick start

English narration transcript. These are illustrated examples, not a live screen recording.

## Meet WindowMochi

WindowMochi remembers where your windows belong. This illustrated guide shows how to save a multi-display layout and bring it back with one shortcut.

## 1. Launch the app

After building the project, open dist slash WindowMochi dot app. It runs in the menu bar, so there is no regular app window. Click the mochi icon to open its menu.

## 2. Allow window control

Choose Grant Accessibility permission. In System Settings, enable WindowMochi under Privacy and Security, Accessibility. You make this permission change yourself. Then return to the app.

## 3. Save your layout

Arrange your windows across your displays. Choose Save current layout, enter a name such as Work, and click Save. You can keep several layouts for different activities.

## 4. Restore in one click

Move or resize a few windows. Open the mochi menu and choose Restore Work. WindowMochi moves your open windows back to their saved displays, positions, and sizes.

## 5. Switch with shortcuts

Hold Control, Option, and Command, then press one through nine to restore a layout. Use the right or left arrow with the same modifiers to cycle through layouts.

## 6. Choose your language

Open Language in the menu to choose English, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, or Japanese. By default, WindowMochi follows your system language preferences.

## Permission still not working?

If the switch is on but window control still fails, quit the app, remove its old Accessibility entry, then add and enable the exact app you run. Reopen it without rebuilding. See the README for details.
